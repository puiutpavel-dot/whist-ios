This version consolidates our Romanian card games into ONE app, following the guidance in the previous 4.3(a) review. "Whist Românesc", "Remi Etalat: Tile Rummy" and "Masa de Renț – Rentz" were separate submissions; they are now three game modes of this single app, and the separate apps will not be resubmitted.

The app is single-player against computer opponents. No account, no login, no network access, no ads, no in-app purchases, no real-money or simulated gambling (no betting, no currency). Languages: Romanian, English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Hungarian (follows the device language).

To try it: the main screen lists the three games. Tap one, then "New game".
- Whist: tap a number to bid, then tap a raised card to play.
- Tile Rummy: draw a tile, select tiles to meld or tap Suggestion, then discard one tile.
- Rentz: when it's your turn to choose, pick one of the 8 games, then tap a raised card to play.
