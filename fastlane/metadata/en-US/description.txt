Five Romanian classics in one app: Whist, tile Rummy (Remi etalat), Rentz, Avioane and Castelul (The Castle). Play the card games solo against the computer, anytime, offline; play Avioane online with a friend too.

WHIST
• Bid exactly how many tricks you'll take; the dealer can't make the bids add up.
• 1-8-1 and 8-1-8 formats, 3–6 players.
• Forehead card in the last one-card hands.
• The coach suggests your bid, with a reason, and the card to play.
• Cards-played tracker and scoreboard.

TILE RUMMY (REMI ETALAT)
• Classic Romanian rules: 45-point opening, trump tile, Jokers, adding to melds and going out.
• 2–4 players, suggestion button for melds.

RENTZ
• All 8 games: King of Hearts, Queens, Diamonds, No Tricks, Totals, Whist, Ten of Clubs and Rentz.
• Each player chooses a game in turn after seeing their hand, with a tip.
• 3–6 players, score table with each player's chosen games.

AVIOANE (PLANES)
• The school-desk classic: hide your planes on a 10×10 grid and hunt your opponent's.
• Water, Hit or Destroyed; a hit on the nose downs a plane instantly.
• 1, 2 or 3 planes per player.
• Against the bot (3 levels) or online with a friend or a random opponent via Game Center.

THE CASTLE (CASTELUL)
• The Romanian playground game with a stone tower and a ball, turned into a card game.
• Build your castle from Ace to 7; balls (8, 9, 10) knock down a top level of the same colour.
• Jacks block, Queens stand in for any level, Kings raise a shield.
• 2–4 players, opponents on 3 levels, a tip every turn.

NO ADS, NO ACCOUNT
No data collected. No betting or money, real or virtual.

Available in English, Romanian, German, French, Italian, Spanish and Hungarian.
