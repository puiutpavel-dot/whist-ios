Romanian whist, the way it is played at the table, now on iPhone. The app is in Romanian.

Play against 2–5 computer opponents with the classic rules:
• 3–6 players, 8 cards per player in the deck
• 1-8-1 or 8-1-8 formats
• trump is turned up after the deal; 8-card hands are played without trump
• the dealer cannot make the total of the bids equal the number of cards
• you must follow suit, and must trump if you can't
• 5 points plus one per trick for an exact contract, minus the difference if you miss

Optional variants:
• Card on the forehead: in the last one-card hands you can't see your own card, only everyone else's
• Streak bonuses: ±10 points for 5 contracts made or missed in a row

Also:
• the classic paper-style score sheet
• the cards you are allowed to play are raised, so you never misplay
• stats: games, wins, best score
• three opponent speeds

No account, no ads, works offline. We collect no data.
